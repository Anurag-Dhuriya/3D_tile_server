How to use the new endpoint:

Test a specific model:
http://localhost:8080/api/citydb/test?model=ahmedabad_city
Keep the exported test tiles on disk for inspection:
http://localhost:8080/api/citydb/test?model=ahmedabad_city&keep=1
What it tells you:

whether citydb-tool is reachable
whether the DB/schema settings are valid
whether tiled CityGML export works
how many test tiles were produced
